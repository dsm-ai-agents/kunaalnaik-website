Claude Cowork for finance

Cowork for finance, traceable by design.

Setup and enablement for finance teams using Claude Cowork on multi-step reporting work — with project instructions, source discipline, and a review gate before anything reaches a board pack.

The problem this solves

Cowork can carry a whole reporting cycle, which is exactly the risk. Without project instructions and source discipline, a fluent board narrative can drift from the workbook it claims to describe.

What the engagement should produce

  • Project instructions encoding your chart of accounts, periods, and reporting conventions
  • A variance and board-pack workflow where every figure traces to a source file
  • A reconciliation review loop that surfaces anomalies for a human decision
  • Connector and file-access boundaries agreed before sensitive data is involved
  • A reviewer checklist covering period alignment, arithmetic, and restatement risk

Ways to work together

  • Cowork demonstration for finance leadership
  • Project setup and instruction sprint
  • Hands-on finance team workshop
  • Reporting-cycle implementation with handover

Who it is for

Finance teams on Claude paid, Team, or Enterprise plans running recurring reporting cycles.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.
