## Search whitespace observed

- `Claude Cowork consultant` has emerging specialist pages, courses, and official education, but limited India-specific enterprise enablement.
- `Claude Cowork training for teams` is increasingly competitive; winning requires role-based workflows, governance, examples, and proof—not product summaries.
- `Hermes Agent training` is dominated by tutorials, beginner courses, and product documentation. There is little visible business implementation consulting.
- `corporate AI training India` contains major consultancies, training companies, and SEO guide pages. A personal brand needs a distinct curriculum, senior-led delivery, and independently checkable proof.
- `AI consulting for SMB/MSME` responds well to direct language: no oversized programme, first high-value workflow, team self-sufficiency, and measurable before/after state.

## Patterns adopted in the base site

1. **Role and buyer segmentation:** enterprise teams versus owner-led SMBs/MSMEs.
2. **Training plus implementation:** capability is connected to a real workflow, not a feature tour.
3. **Simple operating sequence:** Frame → Prototype → Verify → Transfer.
4. **Explicit governance:** data boundaries, approval gates, review, and ownership appear in commercial copy.
5. **Named senior practitioner:** Kunaal is the trainer/consultant, not an anonymous delivery bench.
6. **Email-first scoping:** direct contact instead of a fake booking widget or generic lead form.
7. **Evidence discipline:** uncertain metrics, logos, testimonials, and view counts are removed until verified.
8. **Product wedges, business category:** Claude Cowork and Hermes Agent support the offer; they do not replace the core category of AI training and workflow adoption.
