About Kunaal Naik

From data practitioner to AI operating partner.

Kunaal Naik is the founder of Data Science Masterminds, an AI automation coach, trainer, consultant, and TEDx speaker focused on making advanced AI useful in everyday work.

A practical point of view

Kunaal works at the intersection of data, learning, automation, and business adoption. The through-line is not a particular tool. It is helping people understand complex capability, apply it to meaningful work, and build enough confidence and discipline to use it repeatedly.

His current focus is AI workflow operating systems: the combination of people, context, tools, approval boundaries, reusable procedures, and measurement that turns AI from an isolated experiment into a working capability.

What he teaches

  • How leaders choose valuable, governable AI opportunities
  • How teams describe work and supply context precisely
  • How professionals delegate bounded tasks and supervise AI agents
  • How to verify polished outputs before they become decisions
  • How to preserve stable methods as projects, skills, and automations

Tools are a means, not the offer

Claude Cowork and Hermes Agent are useful wedges because they make multi-step, agentic work tangible. Kunaal also works with AI agents, no-code and low-code automation, n8n, data workflows, and the surrounding operating practices required for responsible adoption.
