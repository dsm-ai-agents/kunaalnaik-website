AI training for marketing

AI for marketing without brand drift.

Training for marketing leaders and content teams on using AI for research, planning, and drafting while keeping brand voice, factual accuracy, and editorial standards under human control.

The problem this solves

Marketing adopts AI fastest and pays for it in brand dilution. Volume rises, voice flattens, claims go out unverified, and the team ends up editing mediocre drafts instead of doing better thinking.

What the engagement should produce

  • A campaign planning workflow that starts from audience evidence, not a blank prompt
  • A brand-voice specification the team and the tools both work from
  • An editorial QC pass covering claims, sources, and originality before publication
  • A research and synthesis loop for positioning and competitive input
  • Measures for output quality, revision cycles, and time from brief to approved asset

Ways to work together

  • Marketing leadership briefing
  • Two-day marketing workflow workshop
  • Brand-voice and editorial standards lab
  • Campaign planning sprint

Who it is for

CMOs, marketing directors, content and brand leads who need leverage without turning the brand into generic AI output.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.

How scope is decided

Send the audience, workflow or business priority, current tools, constraints, and desired outcome. The first response will identify the smallest useful starting point and any missing information—not force a standard package.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Will our content start sounding like everyone else's?

That is the failure mode the programme is built to prevent. The first deliverable is a brand-voice specification, and the editorial QC pass explicitly checks for generic phrasing and unsupported claims.

Do you cover SEO and AI search visibility?

At a working level — how answer engines select and cite sources, and what that means for content structure. Deep technical SEO is a separate consulting engagement.
