Hermes Agent for executives

A persistent agent for executive load.

Setup for senior leaders who want AI that already knows the context — the portfolio, the people, the priorities — and produces briefings and summaries on a schedule rather than on request.

The problem this solves

Executives get the least value from disposable chat because re-establishing context costs more than the answer saves. Persistent memory changes that, provided approval gates prevent an agent from acting in a leader's name.

What the engagement should produce

  • A memory structure holding portfolio, priorities, and stakeholder context
  • A scheduled briefing workflow that reports only what changed
  • Preparation packs for recurring meetings and reviews
  • Explicit approval gates: nothing is sent or committed without confirmation
  • A quarterly review routine to keep memory accurate

Ways to work together

  • Executive setup and architecture session
  • Briefing workflow design sprint
  • Chief-of-staff enablement
  • Quarterly advisory cadence

Who it is for

CEOs, founders, and senior leaders — often with an EA or chief of staff who will co-own the configuration.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.

How scope is decided

Send the audience, workflow or business priority, current tools, constraints, and desired outcome. The first response will identify the smallest useful starting point and any missing information—not force a standard package.
