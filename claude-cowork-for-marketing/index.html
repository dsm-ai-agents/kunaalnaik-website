Claude Cowork for marketing

Cowork for marketing, on brand.

Setup for marketing teams using Cowork across campaign planning and content production, with brand voice encoded in project instructions and an editorial pass before anything publishes.

The problem this solves

Marketing teams get volume from Cowork immediately and lose voice just as fast. Without encoded brand standards and an editorial gate, output converges on generic and the team becomes a copy-editing queue.

What the engagement should produce

  • Project instructions carrying brand voice, audience, and prohibited claims
  • A campaign brief-to-draft workflow with a defined review point
  • An editorial QC pass covering claims, sources, originality, and tone
  • Reusable project structure so each campaign does not start from zero
  • Measures for revision cycles and time from brief to approved asset

Ways to work together

  • Cowork demonstration for marketing leadership
  • Brand-voice instruction sprint
  • Hands-on marketing team workshop
  • Campaign production implementation

Who it is for

Marketing, content, and brand teams on Claude paid, Team, or Enterprise plans producing recurring campaigns.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.

How scope is decided

Send the audience, workflow or business priority, current tools, constraints, and desired outcome. The first response will identify the smallest useful starting point and any missing information—not force a standard package.
