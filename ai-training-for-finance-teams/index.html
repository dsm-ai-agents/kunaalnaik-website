AI training for finance

AI for finance work that survives audit.

Hands-on training for controllers, FP&A, and finance operations. Teams practise on their own reporting cycle and leave with drafted workflows, a review standard, and explicit boundaries on what AI may never decide.

The problem this solves

Finance teams adopt AI for speed and inherit an audit problem. Numbers get restated in prose nobody traced, source workbooks drift from the narrative, and no one can show which figure a sentence came from when the auditor asks.

What the engagement should produce

  • A variance-commentary workflow that cites the source cell or query for every claim
  • A month-end narrative draft loop with a named reviewer and sign-off step
  • A verification checklist covering arithmetic, period alignment, and restatement risk
  • Documented rules for what stays manual: accruals, judgements, disclosures
  • Baseline and after-state measures for close-cycle hours and rework

Ways to work together

  • Half-day finance leadership briefing
  • Two-day FP&A workflow workshop
  • Month-end close workflow lab on live (masked) data
  • Enablement programme with post-close office hours

Who it is for

CFOs, finance controllers, FP&A leads, and shared-service finance teams under reporting pressure who cannot trade traceability for speed.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Will AI be allowed to touch the numbers?

No. The workflows draft commentary and summaries from figures the team has already produced and reconciled. Calculation stays in the ledger and the model; AI drafts the explanation, and every claim carries its source.

Do you work on our real data?

Preferably on masked or prior-period data. The workflow must be realistic; the dataset does not need to be live. Data handling and access are agreed before the session.
