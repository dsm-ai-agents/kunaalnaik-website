## Public positioning

Kunaal Naik is the founder of Data Science Masterminds, a corporate AI trainer, practical AI adoption consultant, AI automation coach, and TEDx speaker.

He works at the intersection of:

- AI agents and agentic workflows
- Data-informed decision-making
- Corporate AI training and implementation
- Workflow automation
- Professional and founder capability-building
- Business adoption, facilitation, and operating systems

His role is to bridge theoretical AI knowledge and useful implementation: understand the business problem, choose the appropriate approach, build a bounded workflow, verify it, and transfer ownership.

## Vision

Make AI accessible, practical, and useful for professionals, founders, and business teams regardless of technical background.

The larger ambition is to help people use AI as an everyday capability for better decisions, less repetitive work, stronger execution, and responsibly created economic value.

## Mission

Train and equip professionals, founders, and business teams to build, deploy, supervise, and improve AI workflows that solve real problems.

Public mission loop:

1. **Understand** — clarify the problem, user, workflow, data, constraints, and desired outcome.
2. **Train** — explain the business logic and AI capability through hands-on application.
3. **Build** — implement the smallest useful workflow rather than stopping at theory.
4. **Verify** — check facts, decisions, data handling, failure modes, and approval boundaries.
5. **Transfer** — document the method, train the owner, and measure whether the work improved.

Monetization and business-building may be discussed for founder and professional audiences, but never as a guaranteed income outcome.

## Teaching and consulting principles

### Practical over perfect

Useful implementation beats theoretical perfection. Start with the smallest version that can expose value, risk, and missing information; improve it after evidence exists.

### Business problem before technology

Business acumen is more important than collecting technical tools. The differentiator is understanding the work, metric, decision, customer, and constraint before choosing AI, automation, or code.

### Build, do not merely consume

Learning AI without applying it creates shallow confidence. Workshops and programmes should use real or realistic work and leave behind an artifact, workflow, decision, or implementation plan.

### Accessibility without oversimplification

People should not need a technical background to begin. Complex concepts should be made understandable while preserving verification, governance, and production discipline.

### Human judgment remains accountable

AI may research, draft, analyse, coordinate, and automate bounded work. People remain responsible for sensitive data, consequential decisions, approvals, and published outcomes.

### Community and proximity

Capability compounds through consistent practice, useful feedback, and proximity to peers and mentors. Kunaal favours direct involvement, cohort learning, and implementation support over passive content alone.

### Permission-based relationships

Marketing and customer communication should be relevant, transparent, and permission-based. Long-term trust matters more than aggressive short-term conversion.

### Continuous learning

Tools change; the durable capability is learning how to frame problems, work with data, supervise AI, evaluate quality, and adapt.

## Audience

### Enterprise teams

Leaders, L&D teams, transformation functions, and business units that need role-based AI capability, governance, useful pilots, and measurable adoption.

### SMBs and MSMEs

Owner-led and growing businesses that need help identifying and implementing one valuable workflow without an oversized transformation programme.

### Professionals

Mid-career professionals, managers, consultants, and operators who want to move from casual chat use to a portfolio of supervised AI workflows.

### Founders

Founders, solopreneurs, coaches, and consultants who want to use AI agents for business operations or package a proven workflow as an ethical service.
