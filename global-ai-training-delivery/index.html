Global delivery

Delivered where the team actually is.

How international engagements work in practice — timezone coverage, onsite and remote formats, contracting and currency, and what changes when a programme spans regions.

The problem this solves

Most trainer sites answer international questions with a list of city pages. That tells a buyer nothing about timezone coverage, contracting, travel, or how a multi-region rollout is actually sequenced.

What the engagement should produce

  • Timezone bands genuinely covered for live delivery, and the limits
  • Format guidance: when onsite justifies travel and when hybrid performs better
  • Contracting and currency options, including invoicing entity and taxes
  • Sequencing for multi-region rollouts so cohorts do not diverge
  • Localisation boundaries: what adapts per region and what stays standard

Ways to work together

  • Remote live delivery across Indian, Gulf, European, UK, and US timezone bands
  • Onsite delivery with travel scoped in the proposal
  • Hybrid programmes pairing remote practice with onsite intensives
  • Multi-region enablement with staggered cohorts and a shared measurement model

Who it is for

Multinational L&D teams, GCCs, and distributed organisations needing consistent AI capability across regions.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

Which timezones can you deliver live?

Indian, Gulf, European, and UK bands comfortably; US East Coast with a scheduling compromise. US West Coast live delivery is limited and usually better served by a hybrid design.

Can you invoice outside India?

Yes. Contracting currency and invoicing entity are agreed during scoping, in INR or USD.

Do you publish a client list by country?

A dated delivery ledger is shared during scoping. Public client naming only happens with written permission, so this page does not list logos.
