Hermes Agent for founders

The founder's agent, scoped honestly.

Setup for founders and small teams who need leverage before they need headcount — starting from the few workflows genuinely worth automating, and refusing the ones that are not.

The problem this solves

Founders automate the interesting problem rather than the expensive one, then maintain an agent that saves less time than it consumes. Selection discipline matters more than capability here.

What the engagement should produce

  • A ranked shortlist of candidate workflows, with the rejected ones and reasons
  • Two or three implemented workflows with named owners and failure routes
  • A cost model covering tokens, model routing, and what to cap
  • Scheduled operations that stay quiet unless something needs attention
  • A stop rule: when to retire an agent instead of maintaining it

Ways to work together

  • Founder setup and prioritisation session
  • Implementation sprint for the first workflows
  • Small-team enablement workshop
  • Advisory as the company scales

Who it is for

Founders, solo operators, and small teams under ten people where the founder is still the bottleneck.

No black-box transformation. The goal is internal capability, explicit ownership, and evidence that the workflow improved the work.

How scope is decided

Send the audience, workflow or business priority, current tools, constraints, and desired outcome. The first response will identify the smallest useful starting point and any missing information—not force a standard package.

Common questions

Before you enquire.

What if none of my workflows are worth automating?

Then the honest answer is to say so, and the session will. Selection is the valuable part; building an agent for unstable, low-frequency work costs more than it returns.

How much does running an agent cost?

It depends on model routing and volume, which is why cost modelling is an explicit deliverable rather than an afterthought.
